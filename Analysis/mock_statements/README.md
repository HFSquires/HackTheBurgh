# Analysis/mock_statements/

Output of `mock_statements.py` (repository root): 100 mock Lloyds-layout statement CSVs (`persona<NNN>_<uuid8>.csv`) (3 months to 31 October 2026) and `personas.md` (persona details, salary source row, modelled monthly spend). Written by code; never edit by hand. Regenerate with `.venv\Scripts\python mock_statements.py`. Merchant names are synthetic flavour. Spending is scaled by gross-income decile (T05b) and uprated to September 2026 prices by CPI division, and net pay uses 2026/27 tax bands (T15), with pay uprated to August 2026 by ONS average weekly earnings (T15b); end balances range from -1,215.34 to 16,435.65 GBP (median 5,222.46; upper median 5,267.42, as printed by `--check`; 3 overdrawn), still on the high side because several ONS spending items are excluded (see `USER_GUIDE.md`).
