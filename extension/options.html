Second Thought

Checks your basket at checkout and looks for the same items cheaper elsewhere.

Your account

Sign in to use the delivery details, payment preferences and budget saved on your account page.

Edit details on your account page

OpenAI or ELM API key

Used to search the web for real prices. Stored only in this browser and sent only to OpenAI, or to the University of Edinburgh's ELM for an ELM key (elm-…). Get an OpenAI key at platform.openai.com/api-keys. Without a key the extension shows sample results.

Default: gpt-6-luna (fastest, cheapest). If it misses matches, try gpt-6.1-terra or gpt-6.1-sol.

Your budget (optional)

Lets the verdict say whether a purchase fits your plan. Leave blank to judge on price only.

Your delivery details (optional)

Filled in for you when you switch to a cheaper store. Anything you've typed on the checkout you came from is used first. Stored only in this browser. Card details are never stored or filled in.

Price watches

Items you chose to wait on. Prices are re-checked twice a day with your API key, and you get a notification when one drops.
