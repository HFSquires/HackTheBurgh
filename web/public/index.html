Connect the extension

  1. Install the Second Thought extension in Chrome.
  2. Open its settings (right-click the toolbar icon → Options).
  3. Click Sign in and use this same account. Your details sync automatically.

Delete your data

Removes everything saved here. The extension keeps working, just without your saved details.
